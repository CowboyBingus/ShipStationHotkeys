Ship Station Hotkeys v1.9 (formerly Galactic Menu Hotkey)
Supports Helldivers 2 Steam build 25480438 / EXE 1.8.46015.0.

Install Bingus Shared Loader v17 or newer. Import this ZIP into Arsenal or
HD2MM, enable both addons, deploy, and restart Helldivers 2. Keep the shared
loader as the winning Wwise startup replacement.
This is the same addon as Galactic Menu Hotkey under a new name; managers
treat it as an update and saved shortcut keys are kept. Remove the withdrawn
Galactic Menu Hotkey v1.3 and v1.4 packages if they are still installed.

While no other menu is open aboard the Super Destroyer:
Tab  Galactic Map             F1  Armory
F5   Control Center           F6  Ship Management
F7   Stratagem Hero (beside cabinet)   F8  Hellpod Deployment

F8 works after you select a mission while you are alone on the ship. It
opens the briefing and seats you in your Hellpod behind it. Pressed while the
pod is still opening, it waits up to ten seconds and then enters.
F2-F4 are left to the game's performance monitor.

Install Mod Bindings Menu v2.0 or newer separately to rebind all six shortcuts on the
MODS tab of Options > Mouse & Keyboard or Controller, choose their activation
type, or assign controller buttons.

Vanilla Plus Megapack bundles Ship Station Hotkeys as an option. Enable either
that option or this standalone addon, not both.

Its section and binding names follow the game's Text Language when a
translation is installed; see TRANSLATING.md in the repository.
